WHAT YOUR WEBSITE CAN DO

Useful details.
A clearer next step.

01

A menu people can browse

Organise dishes by category, show prices clearly and add dietary notes you provide. Search and filters can make a longer menu easier to explore.

02

A reason to visit

Use supplied food photography, your story, address and opening hours to help visitors decide whether the place fits their plans.

03

A simple reservation enquiry

Collect a preferred date, party size and contact details with a configured provider. An enquiry is a request; confirmation comes from your team.

04

Built for the phone in their hand

Readable menus, obvious contact actions and lightweight layouts help guests while they are out and about.

Where the starter scope ends

Online ordering, payment, live table inventory and booking-platform subscriptions are separate integrations. A static enquiry form does not reserve a table automatically.
