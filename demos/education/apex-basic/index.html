04 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good questions welcome.

Here's how a straightforward FAQ section can help visitors find answers without searching through multiple pages.

How do I choose a course?+

Start by choosing a class group or an exam foundation. You can also use the course search to find a subject that matches your interests.

What does a typical lesson include?+

A sample lesson structure includes explaining a concept, working through examples, independent practice and time for questions.

Can I explore course details before enquiring?+

Yes. Select “View course details” on any course card to see a short summary and its example focus areas.

Does this site accept real admissions?+

No. Apex Academy is a fictional portfolio concept. Forms, classes and timetables are demonstrations, not real services or admissions.
