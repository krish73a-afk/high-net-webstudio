08 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before we
get started.

Straight answers, before you commit.

What is included in the website packages?

Basic covers one page with up to five sections. Advanced includes up to three brochure pages; Premium includes up to four. Every package uses supplied content and includes a responsive static website, editable files and one consolidated revision round. Compare the full scope.

How long does a project usually take?

Timing depends on the page count, content readiness, integrations and feedback. Share your preferred completion period in the enquiry; we agree on a realistic schedule after reviewing the brief.

Are domains and hosting included?

No. Domain registration, hosting subscriptions and paid third-party tools are separate. We can discuss suitable options and the setup effort before work begins.

Can I request extra pages or integrations?

Yes. Additional brochure pages start at ₹750 each in the estimator. Form providers, booking services, payments, accounts and other backend work need a separate scope, including any provider charges.

Are revisions included?

One consolidated revision round is included in one-off website and digital-service work. Monthly social content includes one approval round per post. A new direction or extra deliverables receive a separate quote.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. Static websites are delivered as separate HTML, CSS, JavaScript and asset files. Digital tasks include the editable formats agreed in the scope. Third-party subscriptions remain subject to their own terms.

Can my existing website be redesigned?

Send its address and explain what is getting in the way. We will review the structure, content and technology, then confirm whether a focused refresh or a larger redesign is appropriate.

Can you maintain my website?

Static-site maintenance starts at ₹750 per month for two small update requests within one hour total. Larger changes, hosting work and redesigns are quoted separately. See ongoing support.

How do I request a free quotation?

Use the enquiry form below and describe your goal, what you already have and the timing you have in mind. Continue on WhatsApp to review and send your enquiry, prepare an email draft, or copy the enquiry and send it to highnet00@gmail.com.

Are these completed client projects?

The portfolio and Soul Encore are real personal projects. The nine business websites are fictional concepts you can explore. We do not present them as paid client commissions.
