# Starter pricing research

Reviewed 28 October 2026. These are scope-based starter offers selected for this portfolio, not universal market rates or guaranteed earnings. Complexity, skill, source material and revisions affect the appropriate price.

## Sources

- [Gavior published pricing](https://gavior.in/pricing): ₹4,999/month for eight static posts, captions and a content calendar. A useful comparison for a lean content package.
- [EditSlice published pricing](https://editslice.com/pricing): extra post design ₹499 and extra short reel ₹1,999; its studio services and larger packages cover broader support.
- [VideCreators official rate card](https://videcreators.in/GFDH-Portfolio-Rate-Card.pdf): social post ₹400–₹1,500; website handling ₹1,500–₹5,000. The document labels itself as a 2025 portfolio, so it provides context rather than a definitive 2026 average.
- [Freelancer spreadsheet support listing](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/data-management/precise-spreadsheet-data-entry-support): public search-visible listing reported average bids of ₹525/hour. Bids are proposals, not completed transactions or a market average.
- [Plumlet reel pricing comparison](https://plumlet.app/reel-editing-charges-india): search-visible comparison included its ₹499 single-reel offer alongside more expensive work. The comparison page was available in search; the separate pricing endpoint timed out. Use as low-end context, not a verified industry benchmark.

## Selected offers

| Service | Starter price | Deliverable limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data management | ₹750/task | Up to 2 hours; entry of 100 supplied records with 5 fields OR cleanup/organisation of 500 existing rows |
| Video editing | ₹800/video | Up to 60 seconds output from 10 minutes supplied footage; basic cuts, captions, colour balance, one ratio |
| Photo editing | ₹500/set | Five photos; crop, exposure, colour and simple cleanup |
| Social media management | ₹4,500/month | One platform, eight static designs, captions, scheduling and monthly summary |
| Post / thumbnail design | ₹400/design | One concept, one static design and one size; supplied text/assets |
| Landing page | ₹1,500 | Same limited Basic web package, not an additional web tier |
| Website maintenance | ₹750/month | Two small update requests / one hour total on an existing static site |
| Document formatting | ₹600/document | Up to five supplied pages with editable handover and PDF export |

The social starter sits slightly below the cited ₹4,999 package and has one platform with a strict static-post allowance. ₹400 aligns with the low end of the published design rate card. ₹800 targets simple supplied-footage edits rather than complex post-production. Data work has an effort cap and a sample review before accepting a record count. Photo/document offers use small deliverable caps; their exact prices are our own starter decisions rather than asserted market averages.

One consolidated revision round per one-off task; one approval round per social post. Paid assets, ad spend, filming, inbox management, complex motion, data scraping and automation are excluded or quoted separately. Monthly time does not roll over. Confirm scope, timing and total before starting.

## Existing website tiers

₹1,500 / ₹3,000 / ₹5,000 were retained. Included page counts, supplied-content assumptions and one revision round are now explicit. The estimator adds pages beyond the chosen tier. Domains, hosting, copywriting and backend integrations are separate. Landing pages use Basic's scope; maintenance has a separate monthly time allowance.

## Currency display

Added 28 October 2026 at the owner's request. Region selection converts the existing INR prices; it does not create regional market rates or change the underlying web tiers. The public [Frankfurter API](https://frankfurter.dev/) supplied the dated fallback snapshot, with the returned quote dates preserved. Cached or bundled rates keep the selector usable without a successful network request. Converted amounts are approximate and the final project quote is agreed separately.

Every converted unit is rounded first, then included in the estimate total. This avoids discrepancies between the visible item prices and their sum. Decimal arithmetic is used at half-cent boundaries. An INR base estimate of ₹8,500, made up of Premium + two extra pages + both add-ons, stays ₹8,500 in India; its foreign display is the sum of the rounded converted units, rather than a separately rounded conversion of the overall sum.
