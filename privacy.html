YOUR INFORMATION / A PLAIN-LANGUAGE NOTICE

Privacy,
made clear.

Last updated 30 October 2026. This notice explains how High Net Webstudio handles website enquiries and the tools used by this static website.

What an enquiry contains

The form asks for your name, email and project description. You may add a phone number, business name, service, budget and preferred timing. We use this information to assess the brief, reply and discuss a possible project. Please do not send passwords, financial details, sensitive student records or other confidential datasets through this form.

How messages reach the studio

Online form submission is not activated in the supplied configuration. Preparing an email draft opens your own email app; you choose whether to send it. Copying the enquiry keeps the text on your device until you share it.

When online submission is activated, the form posts to Formspree over HTTPS. Formspree processes the submitted fields and technical information such as your IP address for delivery and spam protection, and may process data in the United States. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy. Enquiries are delivered to the business mailbox configured by the owner.

WhatsApp enquiries

Choosing Continue on WhatsApp creates a link containing the enquiry fields you supplied and opens a draft addressed to the studio. You review the draft and choose whether to send it in WhatsApp. This action shares the draft text with WhatsApp; its privacy policy and terms apply. The website does not send a WhatsApp message automatically. The direct WhatsApp contact card uses a general greeting.

WhatsApp Privacy Policy

Use, retention and requests

Enquiry details are used for responding, scoping and managing the work you discuss with us. They are not sold or used to create advertising audiences. We review enquiry information when it is no longer needed for the discussion or project; some records may need to be kept for the agreed work or applicable obligations. You can ask what information we hold, request a correction or request deletion by emailing highnet00@gmail.com. We will explain any information that must be retained.

Website hosting and outside services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy statement. The studio typefaces are hosted locally. Some demo typefaces load from Google Fonts, and some restaurant demo photos load from Unsplash; these providers receive normal web requests. The currency selector requests public reference rates from Frankfurter without sending enquiry fields or detecting your location.

GitHub privacy statement · Google privacy policy · Unsplash privacy policy

Local preferences

The browser may remember your colour theme, pricing region and a dated exchange-rate cache using local storage. Session storage remembers whether the intro played. These values contain no enquiry fields. Clear your browser’s site data to remove them. Internal page links may carry a service, selected concept, package, add-ons or region; names, email, phone and message text are not placed in those internal links. An external WhatsApp draft link includes your enquiry only when you choose the WhatsApp option.

Optional analytics

Analytics is disabled in the supplied configuration. No analytics script is loaded by default.

If the owner enables Plausible, it is configured for aggregate page visits, broad traffic sources and a small set of service/demo/contact actions. Enquiry contents and personal fields are not sent. Query strings and fragments are stripped from measured URLs and referrers. The integration respects the browser’s Do Not Track preference. Read Plausible’s data policy. Account setup and a subscription after the trial are the owner’s responsibility.

Fictional website demonstrations

Demo admission, restaurant and fitness forms are simulations. Their preview controls do not process real enrolments, reservations or payments. An explicitly labelled studio quotation link takes you to the separate High Net Webstudio enquiry form.

Questions
